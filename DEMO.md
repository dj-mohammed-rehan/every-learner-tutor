# Demo video script (about 2 minutes)

Record on a phone, or a desktop browser at phone width. Open the app one minute before so the free host is awake: https://every-learner-tutor.onrender.com

1. **0:00 to 0:15, the problem.** "Most learners have no personal tutor, and apps measure time spent, not learning. This tutor finds the gap, teaches it in your language, and only counts it as learned when you prove it."
2. **0:15 to 0:35, start.** Pick Kannada (or Hindi, Tamil, Telugu) in the top right. The whole screen changes language. Type a topic, for example fractions. Tap Start.
3. **0:35 to 1:00, diagnose.** Answer the 3 quick questions, one of them wrong on purpose. Tap Done. Point at the level chip and the gaps the tutor found.
4. **1:00 to 1:25, teach.** Read the first explanation and its example. Reply with "I did not understand". Show that the tutor tries a different explanation.
5. **1:25 to 1:50, prove it.** Tap "I think I understand. Test me". Answer the new questions, getting one wrong. Show the score, the feedback, and that numbers are checked by code, not by the AI.
6. **1:50 to 2:15, fail then pass.** Tap Back to the lesson, let the tutor re-teach, retake the check and pass. Open the progress screen (chart icon top right) to show the gap closed with proof.
7. **2:15 to 2:30, close.** Mention: 5 languages, free tiers only (Rs 0), automated tests and an eval run (see README), graceful fallback when the AI is busy.

Tips: do one dry run first. If the AI is slow, say so and cut the pause in editing. Keep a recorded backup take.
